Class 34
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 129-130
---------------------
 - Sevā-bhakti-kathā-dhyāna-tapo-yātrādi sādhanam | Mānato dambhato naiva kāryam naiverṣhyayā tathā || 129 ||
 - Spardhayā dveshato naiva na laukika-phalecchayā | Shraddhayā shuddha-bhāvena kāryam prasannatā-dhiyā || 130 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.52
  Shraddhāe sahit ne īrṣhyāe rahit je bhakti kare te amne atishay game chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Ma.11
  Jo dharma, arth ne kām sambandhī je phaḷnī icchā teno tyāg karīne tenā te shubh karma jo Bhagwānnī prasannatāne arthe kare to e ja shubh karma chhe te bhaktirūp thaīne kevaḷ mokshane arthe thāy chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Ma.28
  Jīvne Bhagwānne rājī karyāno upāy to man-karma-vachane Bhagwānnā bhaktnī sevā karvī e ja chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Ma.41
  Jene Parameshwar bhajvā hoy tene Bhagwānnī athavā Bhagwānnā bhaktnī sevā-chākarī maḷe tyāre potānu moṭu bhāgya mānīne sevā karvī. Te paṇ Bhagwānnī prasannatāne arthe ne potānā kalyāṇne arthe bhaktie karīne ja karvī, paṇ koīk vakhāṇe te sāru na karvī.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 131
---------------------
 - Dṛushyo na mānuṣho bhāvo Bhagavati tathā gurau | Māyā-parau yato divyāv-Akshara-Purushottamau || 131 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 3/36
  Sārāmā sāru to ā Bhagwān ne sādhuno sambandh thayo chhe tethī kāī sāru nathī ne tethī kāī sāru samajvānu nathī ne bhūnḍāmā bhūnḍu shu chhe? To ā sādhune viṣhe manushyabhāv āve chhe tethī bīju kāī bhūnḍu nathī.
• Swa. Vato: 4/85
  Moṭā sādhuno avaguṇ nahi hoy ne kāī dosh hashe to tenī Bhagwānne phikar chhe paṇ moṭā sādhuno avaguṇ hashe tenu to pāp vajralep thāshe.

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Pa.7
  Ātyantik pralayne ante Akshardhāmne viṣhe jevu Bhagwānnu swarūp anant aishvarya-teje yukt chhe tevu ne tevu ja pratyaksha manushyarūp Bhagwānne viṣhe jāṇvu; teṇe tattve karīne Bhagwānne jāṇyā kahevāy.
• Swa. Vato: 5/66
  Bhagwānnī mūrti dekhvāmā to manushya sarkhī ja āvtī hoy, paṇ e atishay alaukik mūrti chhe. Bhagwān Akshardhāmmā chhe tevā ne tevā ja āvyā chhe, temā bāḷ, joban ne vṛuddhapṇu evā manushyabhāv nathī ne tirodhān nathī, ne manushyabhāv dekhāy chhe te naṭnī māyānī peṭhe chhe.

Kirtan
------
• Premanand Swami
  Mārī mūrati re, mārā lok bhog ne mukta; Sarve divya chhe re, tyā to joyānī chhe jukta... 2

Satsang Diksha Shlok 132
---------------------
 - Vishvāsah sudṛuḍhī-kāryo Bhagavati tathā gurau | Nirbalatvam parityājyam dhāryam dhairyam Harer-balam || 132 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Sā.9
  Jo sant tathā Parameshwarnā vachanne viṣhe atishay shraddhā hoy ane ati dṛuḍh vishwās hoy to game tevā tāmasī karma hoy paṇ teno nāsh thaī jāy ane kaḷiyugnā dharma maṭīne satyayugnā dharma hoy te varte. Māṭe ati sāche bhāve karīne satsang kare to tene koī jātno dosh haiyāmā rahe nahī ane deh chhatā ja brahmarūp thaī jāy.

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 1/126
  Bājaro khāvo ne Prabhu bhajvā, bīju kāī karvu nathī ne roṭlā to Bhagwānne devā chhe, sādhune devā chhe, te deshe, deshe ne deshe.

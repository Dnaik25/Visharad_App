Class 54
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 201-202
---------------------
 - Prashāstā pālayed dharmān niyatā ye sushāsane | Lokānām bharaṇam puṣhṭim kuryāt samskārarakshaṇam || 201 ||
 - Svāsthyashikshaṇasamrakshā-vidyudannajalādikaiḥ | Suvyavasthā vidhātavyā sarvā'bhyudayahetunā || 202 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 203
---------------------
 - Guṇasāmarthyaruchyādi viditvaiva janasya tu | Taduchiteṣhu kāryeṣhu yojanīyo vichārya saḥ || 203 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 204-205
---------------------
 - Shakyā bhagavato yatra bhaktiḥ svadharmapālanam | Tasmin deshe nivāso hi karaṇīyaḥ sukhena cha || 204 ||
 - Vidyādhanādikam prāptum deshāntaram gate'pi cha | Satsangamādarāt tatra kuryānniyamapālanam || 205 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.29
  Pavitra deshmā rahevu ane bhūnḍo kāḷ vartato hoy tyāthī āghumpāchhu khasī nīsarvu, ane sang paṇ prabhunā bhakta ane panch vartamāne yukta evā je brahmavettā sādhu teno karvo, to haribhaktane parameshwarnī je bhakti tenu baḷ atishay vṛuddhi pāme.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 206
---------------------
 - Yaddeshe hi svavāsaḥ syāt taddeshaniyamāshcha ye | Sarvathā pālanīyāste tatprashāsanasammatāḥ || 206 ||

Class 70
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 291
---------------------
 - Aksharabrahmasādharmyam samprāpya dāsabhāvataḥ | Puruṣhottamabhaktirhi muktirātyantikī matā || 291 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.3
  Brahma thakī parabrahma je puruṣhottam nārāyaṇ te nokhā chhe ne e brahmanā paṇ kāraṇ chhe ne ādhār chhe ne prerak chhe, em samajīne potānā jīvātmāne e brahma sangāthe ekatā karīne parabrahmanī svāmī-sevakbhāve upāsanā karvī.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 292
---------------------
 - Samkshipyā'tra kṛutam hyevam āgnopāsanavarṇanam | Tadvistaram vijānīyāt sāmpradāyikashāstrataḥ || 292 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 293-294
---------------------
 - Etatsatsangadīksheti shāstrasya prativāsaram | Kāryaḥ satsangibhiḥ pāṭha ekāgrachetasā janaiḥ || 293 ||
 - Paṭhane chā'samarthaistu shravyam tat prītipūrvakam | Ācharitum cha kartavyaḥ prayatnaḥ shraddhayā tathā || 294 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 295-296
---------------------
 - Paramātmā param brahma svāminārāyaṇo hariḥ | Siddhāntam sthāpayāmāsa hyaksharapuruṣhottamam || 295 ||
 - Guravashcha guṇātītāshchakrustasya pravartanam | Virachitamidam shāstram tatsiddhāntā'nusārataḥ || 296 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 297-298
---------------------
 - Kṛupayaivā'vatīrṇo'tra mumukṣhumokṣhahetunā | Sakalā' 'shritabhaktānām yogakshemau tathā'vahat || 297 ||
 - Vyadhāt sa dvividham shreya āmuṣhmikam tathaihikam || 298 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ka.5
  Potānā je premī bhakta tenā manorath pūrā karvā e ja bhagwānne avatār dharyānu prayojan chhe.

Kirtan
------
• Nishkulanand Swami
  Je je harie karyu het, evu kare koṇ āpaṇe re;
  Māt tāt sagā samet, mānyā sanehī bhoḷāpaṇe re... 1
  Jone garbhvāsabī trās, ṭaḷī ṭaḷe kem koynī re;
  Te paṇ ṭāḷīne avināsh, rākhe khabar anna toynī re... 2
  Vaḷī same same sambhāḷ, jāṇo kare harijanne re;

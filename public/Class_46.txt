Class 46
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 169
---------------------
 - Sukhe nodrichyate yash-cha duhkhe chodvijate na hi | Swāminārāyaṇecchātah sarvam pravartate yatah || 169 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 170
---------------------
 - Vivādah kalaho vā'pi naiva kāryah kadāchana | Vartitavyam vivekena rakshyā shāntish-cha sarvadā || 170 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 171
---------------------
 - Kaṭhoratām bhajen-naiva janah ko'pi kadāchana || 171 ||

======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 229
---------------------
 - Bhaved yo dṛuḍhaniṣhṭhāvān aksharapuruṣhottame | Dṛuḍhabhaktirvivekī cha kuryāt tatsangamādarāt || 229 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa.Vato.1/177
  Baḷiyā bhagwānnā bhakta hoy teno sang karīe to jīvmā baḷ āve.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 230
---------------------
 - Harerguroshcha vākyeṣhu shankā yasya na vidyate | Vishvāsurbuddhimān yashcha kuryāt tatsangamādarāt || 230 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 231
---------------------
 - Āgnāyāḥ pālane nityam sotsāham tatparo dṛuḍhaḥ | Nirmānaḥ saralo yashcha kuryāt tatsangamādarāt || 231 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 232
---------------------
 - Harerguroshcharitreṣhu divyeṣhu mānuṣheṣhu yaḥ | Sasneham divyatādarshī kuryāt tatsangamādarāt || 232 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 233
---------------------
 - Tatparo'nyaguṇagrāhe vimukho durguṇoktitaḥ | Suhṛudbhāvī cha satsange kuryāt tatsangamādarāt || 233 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 234
---------------------
 - Lakshyam yasyaikamātram syād guruhariprasannatā | Āchāre'pi vichāre'pi kuryāt tatsangamādarāt || 234 ||

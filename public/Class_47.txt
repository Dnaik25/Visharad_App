Class 47
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 172
---------------------
 - Sevām mātuh pituh kuryād gṛuhī satsangam-āshritah | Pratidinam namaskāram tat-pādeshu nivedayet || 172 ||

Taittiriya Upanishad
--------------------
• Taittiriya Upanishad - 1/11/2
  Mātṛudevo bhava. Pitṛudevo bhava.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 173
---------------------
 - Shvashurah pitṛu-vat sevyo vadhvā shvashrūsh-cha mātṛu-vat | Sva-putrī-vat snushā pālyā shvashrvā'pi shvashureṇa cha || 173 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 174
---------------------
 - Sampālyāh putra-putryash-cha satsanga-shikshaṇādinā | Anye sambandhinah sevyā yathā-shakti cha bhāvatah || 174 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 175
---------------------
 - Gṛuhe hi madhurām vāṇīm vaded vācham tyajet kaṭum | Kam-api pīḍitam naiva prakuryān-malinā'shayāt || 175 ||

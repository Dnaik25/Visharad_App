Class 42
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 159
---------------------
 - Yāvantah syur-gruhasthāsh-cha tyāgino vā nija-āshrame | Pālayeyur-nijān dharmān yathā-shāstra-vinirṇayam || 159 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.51
  Jeṭlā tyāgī chhe tene to tyāgīnā dharma pramāṇe vartvu ane jeṭlā gṛuhasth haribhakt chhe temne gṛuhasthnā dharma pramāṇe vartvu ane jeṭlī bāīo haribhakt chhe tene bāīonā dharma pramāṇe vartvu ane jo te thakī ochhu varte to paṇ sukh na thāy ane jo te thakī adhik varte to paṇ sukh na thāy. Shā māṭe je, Parameshwarnā kahel je dharma te pramāṇe ja granthmā lakhyu hoy, temā koī rīte bādh āve evu na hoy ne sukhe paḷe evu hoy ne tethī ochhu-adhiku karvā jāy, te karnāro jarūr duhkhi thāy.

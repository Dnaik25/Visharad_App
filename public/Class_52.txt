Class 52
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 187
---------------------
 - Sangash-chāritrya-hīnāyāh karaṇīyo nahi striyāh | Strībhih sva-dharma-rakshārtham pālyāsh-cha niyamā dṛuḍham || 187 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 188
---------------------
 - Na tādṛuk-shṛuṇuyād vācham gītam grantham paṭhen-na cha | Yena kāma-vṛuddhih syāt tādṛug dṛushyam na chekshayet || 188 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.35
  Game tevo samādhinishṭh hoy athavā vichārvān hoy ne te jo strīonā prasangmā man-bhanḍe to eno dharma koī rīte rahe ja nahī; ane game tevī dharmavāḷī strī hoy ne tene jo purushno sahavās thāy to eno paṇ dharma rahe ja nahī.
• Vach.Ga.Pr.70
  Antarmā je kusangī rahyā chhe ne bahār rahyā chhe te be ek chhe...Māṭe bahārnā kusangīno sang na kare ne bahārnā je sant chhe teno ja sang rākhe to kusangīnu baḷ ghaṭī jāy ne santnu baḷ vadhe em chhe.
• Vach.Lo.1
  Kāmnu mūḷ ūkhaḍī jāy topaṇ brahmacharyādik niyammānthī koī rīte ḍagvu nahi.

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 5/99
  Gāmne gaḍh hoy tem āpaṇe panchvartamānrūpī gaḍh chhe ne temā thāṇāne ṭhekāṇe niyam chhe, ne jem thāṇu gaḍhne sāchve tem niyam vartamānne sāchve chhe māṭe jeṭlā niyam moḷā paḍe eṭlā phānkā paḍyā jāṇvā.

Haricharitramrutsagar
---------------------
• Haricharitramrutsagar
  Satsangmā evī rīti hatī ke, dash varshnī chhokrī hoy ane dash varshno chhokro hoy, topaṇ bhegā besī rame nahī.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 189
---------------------
 - Dhana-dravya-dharādīnām sadā'dāna-pradānayoh | Niyamā lekha-sākshyādeh pālanīyā avashyatah || 189 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 190
---------------------
 - Prasange vyavahārasya sambandhibhir-api svakaih | Lekhādi-niyamāh pālyāh sakalair-āshritair-janaih || 190 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 191
---------------------
 - Na kāryo vyavahārash-cha duṣhṭair-janaih saha kvachit | Dīna-janeshu bhāvyam cha satsangibhir-dayā'nvitaih || 191 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.72
  Bhagwānno bhakta hoy athavā koī bījo hoy, paṇ garībmātrane leshmātra dukhvavo nahī. Ane jo garībne dukhve to tenu koī prakāre sāru thāy nahī ane jo garībne dukhve to brahmahatyā jeṭlu pāp thāy chhe.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 192
---------------------
 - Laukikam tv-avichāryaiva sahasā karma nā'charet | Phalādikam vichāryaiva vivekena tad ācharet || 192 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.11
  Jo dharma, arth ne kām sambandhī je phaḷnī icchā teno tyāg karīne tenā te shubh karma jo Bhagwānnī prasannatāne arthe kare to e ja shubh karma chhe te bhaktirūp thaīne kevaḷ mokshane arthe thāy chhe.

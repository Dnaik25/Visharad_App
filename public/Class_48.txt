Class 48
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 176
---------------------
 - Militvā bhojanam kāryam gṛuhasthaih sva-gṛuhe mudā | Atithir-hi yathā-shakti sambhāvya āgato gṛuham || 176 ||

Taittiriya Upanishad
--------------------
• Taittiriya Upanishad - 1/11/2
  Atithidevo bhava.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 177
---------------------
 - Maraṇādi-prasangeshu kathā-bhajana-kīrtanam | Kāryam visheshatah smāryo hy-Akshara-Purushottamah || 177 ||

Title: Antakale dhamma lai javanu Swaminarayan Bhagwannu vardan chhe.

Bhaktichintamani
----------------
• Bhaktichintamani - 68/9
  Mārā janne antakāḷe, jarūr māre āvvu; Birud māru e na badle, te sarve janne jaṇāvvu.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 178
---------------------
 - Putrī-putrātmikā svasya samskāryā santatih sadā | Satsanga-divya-siddhāntaih sadāchāraish-cha sad-guṇaih || 178 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 179
---------------------
 - Satsanga-shāstra-pāṭhādyair-garbha-sthām-eva santatim | Samskuryāt pūrayen nishṭhām Akshara-Purushottame || 179 ||

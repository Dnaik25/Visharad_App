Class 42
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 159
---------------------
 - Ādareṇa praṇāmaish-cha madhura-vachanādibhihi | Yatho-chitam hi sanmānyā vruddhā gnāna-vayo-guṇaihi || 159 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 160
---------------------
 - Sadaivā'daraṇīyā hi vidvad-varishṭha-shikshakāh | Yathā-shakti cha satkāryāh sādhu-vādādi-karmaṇā || 160 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 161
---------------------
 - Jana-sambodhanam kuryād yathā-kārya-guṇādikam | Samvardhayet tad-utsāham yathā-shakti su-karmasu || 161 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 162
---------------------
 - Satyām vaded hitām chaiva vaded vāṇīm priyām tathā | Mithyā'ropyo'pavādo na kasmimsh-chit karhichij-jane || 162 ||

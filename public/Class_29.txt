Class 29
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 107
---------------------
 - Brahmā'kshara-guru-dvārā Bhagavān prakaṭah sadā | Sahitah sakalaishvaryaih paramā'nandam-arpayan ॥107॥

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Sā.10
  Evā santnu darshan thayu tyāre em jāṇavu je, 'Mane sākshātkār Bhagwānnu darshan thayu.'
• Vach.Ga.An.26
  Evā sant manushya chhe to paṇ Bhagwānnī peṭhe sevā karvā yogya chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Pa.68
  Mārī ashṭ prakārnī je pratimā tathā je sant tene vishe hu akhand nivās karīne rahu chhu.
• Vach.Va.19
  Ā jīvne jyāre Bharatkhaṇḍne vishe manushya deh āve chhe tyāre Bhagwānnā avatār kā Bhagwānnā sādhū e jarūr pṛuthvī upar vichartā hoy; tenī jo e jīvne oḷkhāṇ thāy to e jīv Bhagwānno bhakta thāy chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Ma.22
  Sādhu thakī koī bījī moṭī padvī nathī. Jem rājā hoy ne tenī rāṇī hoy, te jeṭlāmā rājānu rājya chhe teṭlāmā rāṇīnu rājya paṇ kahevāy ane jevo rājāno hukam chāle tevo ja rāṇīno paṇ hukam chāle; tem Bhagwānno jevo pratāp chhe tevo ja e sādhuno paṇ pratāp chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Pa.27
  Ane e samarth to kevo je, enā netramā Bhagwān jonārā chhe te māṭe brahmānḍmā jeṭlā jīvprāṇī chhe tenā netrane prakāsh karvāne samarth thāy chhe, ane enā pagmā chālnārā Bhagwān chhe te māṭe brahmānḍmā sarva jīvnā pagne vishe chālvānī shaktine poshaṇ karvāne e samarth thāy chhe; em e santnī sarve indriyomā Bhagwān rahyā chhe, te māṭe e sant to brahmānḍmā sarve jīvonā indriyone prakāsh karvāne samarth thāy chhe. Māṭe e sant to sarva jagatnā ādhārrūp chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Pa.37
  Evī niṣhṭhāvāḷo je sant chhe tenā pagnī rajne to ame paṇ māthe chaḍhāvīe chhīe, ane tene dukhvatā thakā manmā bīe chhīe, ane enā darshanne paṇ ichchhīe chhīe...je evā yathārth Bhagwānnā bhakta chhe tenu darshan to Bhagwānnā darshan tulya chhe, ane enā darshane karīne anant patit jīvno uddhār thāy chhe evā e moṭā chhe.

Bhaktichintamani
----------------
• Bhaktichintamani - 164/6
  Prakaṭ vāto prakaṭ vratamān re, Prakaṭ bhakta prakaṭ Bhagwān re; Nathī udhārānī eke vāt re, Je je joīe te te sākshāt re.

Purushottam Prakash
-------------------
• Purushottam Prakash - 41/9
  Sant hu ne hu te vaḷī sant re, Em Shrīmukhe kahe Bhagwant re; Sant māanjo mārī mūrti re, Temā pher nathī ek rati re.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 108
---------------------
 - Prītih kāryā'tmabuddhish-cha Brahmā'kshare gurau dṛuḍhā | Pratyaksha-Bhagavad-bhāvāt sevyo dhyeyah sa-bhaktitah || 108 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Va.11
  Satpurushne viṣhe dṛuḍh prīti e ja ātmadarshannu sādhan chhe ane satpurushno mahimā jāṇyānu paṇ e ja sādhan chhe ane Parameshwarnu sākshāt darshan thavānu paṇ e ja sādhan chhe.
• Vach.Ga.An.2
  Jevī paroksh devne viṣhe jīvne pratīti chhe tevī jo pratyaksha gururūp Harine viṣhe āve, to jeṭlā arth prāpt thavānā kahyā chhe teṭlā sarve arth tene prāpt thāy chhe.

Bhaktinidhi
-----------
• Bhaktinidhi - 9/3
  Bhakti saras sahu kahe, paṇ bhakti bhaktimā bhed; Bhakti Prabhu pragaṭnī, em vade chhe chāre ved.

Class 71
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 300
---------------------
 - Sarveṣhām sarvaduḥkhāni tāpatrayamupadravāḥ | Kleshāstathā vinashyeyuragnānam samshayā bhayam || 300 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 301
---------------------
 - Bhagavatkṛupayā sarve svāsthyam nirāmayam sukham | Prāpnuvantu parām shāntim kalyāṇam paramam tathā || 301 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 302
---------------------
 - Na kashchit kasyachit kuryād droham dveṣham tathā janaḥ | Sevantāchāram sarve sarvadaiva parasparam || 302 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 303
---------------------
 - Sarveṣhām jāyatām prītirdṛuḍhā niṣhṭhā cha nishchayaḥ | Vishvāso vardhatām nityam aksharapuruṣhottame || 303 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa.Vato.1/72
  Mahārāj kahe je, karoḍ vahāṇe karīne ek manvār bharāy evī so karoḍ manvāryu bharvī chhe, eṭlā jīvnu kalyāṇ karvu chhe.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 304
---------------------
 - Bhavantu balinaḥ sarve bhaktāshcha dharmapālane | Āpnuyuḥ sahajānanda-parātmanaḥ prasannatām || 304 ||

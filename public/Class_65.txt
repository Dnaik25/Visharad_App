Class 65
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 264-266
---------------------
 - Ādityachandrayorgrahakāle satsangibhiḥ samaiḥ | Parityajya kriyāḥ sarvāḥ kartavyam bhajanam hareḥ || 264 ||
 - Nidrām cha bhojanam tyaktvā tadaikatropavishya cha | Kartavyam grāhamuktyantam bhagavatkīrtanādikam || 265 ||
 - Grāhamuktau savastram hi kāryam snānam samairjanaiḥ | Tyāgibhishcha hariḥ pūjyo deyam dānam gṛuhasthitaiḥ || 266 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 267
---------------------
 - Janmano maraṇasyā'pi vidhayaḥ sūtakādayaḥ | Satsangarītimāshritya pālyāḥ shrāddhādayastathā || 267 ||

Bhaktichintamani
----------------
• Bhaktichintamani - 68/9
  Mārā janne antakāḷe, jarūr māre āvvu; Birud māru e na badle, te sarve janne jaṇāvvu.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 268
---------------------
 - Prāyashchittamanuṣhṭheyam jāte tvayogyavartane | Paramātmaprasādārtham shuddhena bhāvatastadā || 268 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 269
---------------------
 - Āpatkāle tu satyeva hyāpado dharmamācharet | Alpāpattim mahāpattim matvā dharmam na santyajet || 269 ||

Kirtan
------
• Brahmanand Swami 
  Harijan sāchā re, je urmā himmat rākhe,
  Vipatti varatī re, kedī dīn vachan nav bhākhe... 1
  Jagnu sukhduḥkh re, māyik mithyā karī jāṇe,
  Tan dhan jātā re, antarmā shok na āṇe... 2
  Par upkārī re, jan premniyammā pūrā,
  Daihik duḥkhmā re, dāzhe nahi sādhu shūrā... 3
  Harine samare re, nitya ahonish umang bhariyā,
  Sarva tajīne re, naṭnāgar vahālā kariyā... 4
  Brahmānand kahe re, evā harijannī balihārī,
  Mastak jātā re, nav mele ṭek vichārī... 5

Satsang Diksha Shlok 270-271
---------------------
 - Āpattau kaṣhṭadāyām tu rakshā svasya parasya cha | Yathaiva syāt tathā kāryam rakshatā bhagavadbalam || 270 ||
 - Āpattau prāṇanāshinyām prāptāyām tu vivekinā | Gurvādeshā'nusāreṇa prāṇān rakshet sukham vaset || 271 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 272-273
---------------------
 - Satsangarītimāshritya gurvādeshā'nusārataḥ | Parishuddhena bhāvena sarvaiḥ satsangibhirjanaiḥ || 272 ||
 - Desham kālamavasthām cha svashaktimanusṛutya cha | Āchāro vyavahārashcha prāyashchittam vidhīyatām || 273 ||

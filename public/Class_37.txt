Class 37
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 141-142
---------------------
 - Paramātma-Parabrahma-Swāminārāyaṇa-Prabhoh | Brahmā'kshara-svarūpasya Guṇātīta-guros-tathā || 141 ||
 - Tad-arpitasya divyasya siddhāntasya cha sarvadā | Bhaktānām tach-chritānām cha paksho grāhyo vivekatah || 142 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.An.7
  Bhagwānnā bhaktano paksh rākhatā thakā ābarū vadho athavā ghaṭo, athavā mān thāo ke apamān thāo, athavā deh jīvo ke maro, paṇ koī rīte Bhagwān ne Bhagwānnā bhaktano paksh mūkvo nahī ne emno abhāv āvvā devo nahī.
• Vach.Ga.Pr.78
  Jevo dehnā sambandhīno paksh chhe tevo jene satsangno paksh hoy teno pāyo satsangne viṣhe achaḷ chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Ma.60
  Je Bhagwānno bhakta hoy tene koīk mārī nānkhto hoy athavā tene koīk duhkha deto hoy ne te Bhagwānnā bhakta āḍo jaīne jo mare ke ghāyal thāy, to shāstramā em kahyu chhe je, 'Tenā brahmahatyādik je panch mahāpāp te sarve maṭī jāy chhe;' evo Bhagwānnā bhaktano paksh rākhyāno pratāp chhe.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 143-144
---------------------
 - ...gnātvā tad-anuvṛuttim cha tām-evā'nusared dṛuḍham || 143 ||
 - Tad-āgnām pālayet sadya ālasyādi vihāya cha | Sānandotsāha-māhātmym tat-prasāda-dhiyā sadā || 144 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 3/31
  Āgnā ne upāsanā e be pānkho chhe, tene mūkvī ja nahi, to saheje ja Akshardhāmmā javāshe emā kāī sanshay nathī.
• Swa. Vato: 2/49
  Sant kahe tem karvu te shreshṭha chhe ne mandhāryu karvu te kanishṭha chhe, ne mangamtu karto hoy ne te tyāg rākhto hoy ne ākhā mandirnu kām eklo karto hoy ne game eṭlā māṇasne satsang karāvto hoy, to paṇ te nyūn chhe ne tene koīk divas vighna chhe. Ane je traṇ ṭāṇā khāto hoy ne āḷasu hoy ne ūnghto hoy evī rītnā doshe yukt hoy, paṇ jo te potānu mangamtu mūkīne sant kahe tem kare to te adhik chhe. Ne sant kahe em karvu e nirguṇ chhe ne mangamtu karvu e saguṇ chhe.

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.51
  Satpurushnī āgnāne viṣhe varte chhe te ja ātmasattārūpe varte chhe.
• Vach.Ka.11
  Jene Bhagwānne viṣhe prīti hoy te Bhagwānnī āgnā koī kāḷe lope nahī. Jem Bhagwānnu gamtu hoy tem ja rahe e prītinu lakshaṇ chhe.

Yogigita
--------
• Yogigita
  Āpaṇe āgnā upar tān rākhvu. Pragaṭnī ek āgnā bījī karoḍ āgnā kartā vadhāre chhe.

Vachanvidhi
-----------
• Vachanvidhi - 2/9
  Vachane nivṛutti vachane pravṛutti, Vachane baddha mukta kahiye; Te vachan Shrīhari mukhnā, Sukhadāyak sarve lahiye.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 145
---------------------
 - Kim kartum-āgato'smīha kim kurve'ham-iheti cha | Pratidinam sthira-chitte antar-dṛuṣhṭim samācharet || 145 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 2/34
  Nirantar sarva kriyāmā pāchhu vāḷīne jovu je, māre Bhagwān bhajvā chhe ne hu shu karu chhu? Em joyā karvu.

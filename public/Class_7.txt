Class 7
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 17
---------------------
 - Sarve’dhikāriṇo mokṣhe gṛuhiṇas-tyāgino’pi cha । Na nyūnā’dhikatā tatra sarve bhaktā yatah Prabhoho ॥ 17 ॥
 - Householders and renunciants are all entitled to moksha. Between them neither is inferior or superior, because house- holders and renunciants are all devotees of Bhagwan. (17)

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.8
  “...jene Bhagwānnī prāpti thaī chhe ane gṛuhasthāshramī chhe to paṇ te nirguṇ kahevāy ane tyāgī hoy te paṇ nirguṇ kahevāy.”

• Vach.Va.20
  “...gṛuhī-tyāgīno kāī meḷ nathī, jenī samajaṇ moṭī tene ja sauthī moṭo haribhakta jāṇavo.”

Satsang Diksha Shlok 18
---------------------
 - Swāminārāyaṇe’nanya-dṛuḍha-parama-bhaktaye । Gṛuhītvā’shraya-dīkṣhāyā mantram satsangam āpnuyāt ॥ 18 ॥
 - To offer singular, resolute and supreme devotion to  Bhagwan Swaminarayan, one should receive the Ashray Diksha Mantra 3 and affiliate with the Satsang. (18)

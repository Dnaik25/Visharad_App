Class 30
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 109-110
---------------------
 - Swāminārāyaṇo mantro divyash-chā'laukikah shubhah | Japyo'yam sakalair-bhaktair-datto'yam Hariṇā swayam || 109 || | Aksharam Brahma vigneyam mantre Swāmīti shabdatah | Nārāyaṇeti shabdena tat-parah Purushottamah || 110 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 1/153
  Swāminārāyaṇ nāmnā mantra jevo bījo koī mantra āj baḷiyo nathī ne e mantre kāḷā nāgnu paṇ jher na chaḍe ne e mantre viṣhay ūḍī jāy chhe, brahmarūp thāy chhe ne kāḷ, karma, māyānu bandhan chhūṭī jāy chhe, evo bahu baḷiyo e mantra chhe. Māṭe nirantar bhajan karvu.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 111-114
---------------------
 - Swāminārāyaṇeneha siddhānto'yam prabodhitah | Gurubhish-cha Guṇātītair-digante'yam pravartitah || 111 || | Yagnapurushadāsena sthāpito mūrtimattayā | Gurucharitra-grantheshu punar-ayam dṛuḍhāyitah || 112 || | Pramukha-guruṇā yo'yam svīyā'ksharaix sthirīkṛutah | Sākshād guroh prasangena labhyate'yam hi jīvane || 113 || | Ayam-eva sa siddhānto muktipradah sanātanah | Uchyate darshanam divyam Akshara-Purushottamam || 114 ||

Shastriji Maharajno Patra
-------------------------
• Shāstrījī Mahārāje prasangopātt uchchārelā chār mahāvākyo:
  (1) Āpaṇe to Aksharpurushottamnā baḷadiyā chhīe.
  (2) Aksharpurushottam māṭe āpaṇe vechāvu paḍe to paṇ te ochhu chhe.
  (3) Aksharpurushottamni vāt koī mārā māthā par besīne kare toy ochhu chhe.
  (4) Ā munḍāvyu chhe te Aksharpurushottam māṭe munḍāvyu chhe.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 115
---------------------
 - Siddhāntam paramam divyam etādṛusham vichintayan | Satsangam nishṭhayā kuryād ānandotsāha-pūrvakam || 115 ||

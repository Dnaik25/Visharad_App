Class 51
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 184
---------------------
 - Āpat-kāle'nya-rakshārtham sparshe dosho na vidyate | Anyathā niyamāh pālyā anāpattau tu sarvadā || 184 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 185-188
---------------------
 - Ashlīlam yatra dṛushyam syād dharma-samskāra-nāshakam | Nāṭaka-chala-chitrādi tan-na pashyet kadāchana || 185 ||
 - Manusyo vyasanī yah syān-nirlajjo vyabhichāravān | Tasya sango na kartavyah satsangam-āshritair-janaih || 186 ||
 - Sangash-chāritrya-hīnāyāh karaṇīyo nahi striyāh | Strībhih sva-dharma-rakshārtham pālyāsh-cha niyamā dṛuḍham || 187 ||
 - Na tādṛuk-shṛuṇuyād vācham gītam grantham paṭhen-na cha | Yena kāma-vṛuddhih syāt tādṛug dṛushyam na chekshayet || 188 ||

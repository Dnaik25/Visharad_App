Class 57
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 221
---------------------
 - Gurvāshrayavirodhī yo vaidikashāstrakhaṇḍakaḥ | Bhaktimārgavirodhī syāt tasya sangam na chā'charet || 221 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 222
---------------------
 - Na sevyo bhaktihīnashchechchhāstrapārangato'pi vā || 222 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.37
  Amārī sevā kartā hoy ne tenā hṛudaymā jo parameshwarnī bhakti na hoy to te upar het karīe toy paṇ na thāy.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 223
---------------------
 - Shraddhāmeva tiraskṛutya hyādhyātmikeṣhu kevalam | Puraskaroti yastarkam tatsangamācharennahi || 223 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 224
---------------------
 - Satsange'pi kusango yo gneyaḥ so'pi mumukṣhubhiḥ | Tatsangashcha na kartavyo haribhaktaiḥ kadāchana || 224 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa.Vato.5/311
  Satsangmā kusangno jog thāy ne brahmarūp hoy to dehrūp karī nākhe ne satsangmā sārāno jog thāy to dehrūp hoy tene brahmarūp kare.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 225
---------------------
 - Harau gurau cha pratyakshe manuṣhyabhāvadarshanaḥ | Shithilo niyame yashcha na tasya sangamācharet || 225 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 226
---------------------
 - Doṣhadṛuṣhṭishcha bhakteṣhu avaguṇamātrabhāṣhakaḥ | Manasvī gurudrohī cha tasya sangam parityajet || 226 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.An.12
  Bhagwān tathā bhagwānnā bhakta teno jene avaguṇ āvyo hoy ne te satsangī kahevāto hoy to paṇ tene haḍkāyā shvān jevo jāṇvo. Jem haḍkāyā shvānnī lāḷ jene aḍe tene paṇ haḍkavā hāle; tem jene bhagwān ne bhagwānnā bhaktano avaguṇ āvyo hoy te sāthe je het rākhe athavā tenī vāt sāmbhaḷe, to te hetno karnāro ne vātno sāmbhaḷnāro paṇ vimukh sarkho thāy.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 227
---------------------
 - Satkāryanindako yashcha sachchhāstranindako janaḥ | Satsanganindako yashcha tatsangamācharennahi || 227 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 228
---------------------
 - Vachanānām shruteryasya niṣhṭhāyā bhanjanam bhavet | Gurau harau cha satsange tasya sangam parityajet || 228 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Lo.1
  Jene potāne nishchay karāvyo hoy ne te ja vaḷī em kahe je e Bhagwān nathī tyāre ene em jāṇe je ‘enu māthu faryu chhe.’ Evo je hoy tene uttam gnānvāḷo kahīe.

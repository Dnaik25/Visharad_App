Class 38
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 146
---------------------
 - Samprāpyā'kshara-rūpatvam bhajeyam Purushottamam | Pratyaham chintayed-evam svīya-lakshyam-atandritah || 146 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.21
  Āpaṇā satsangī sarvene to em ja nishchay karvo je, 'Āpaṇe paṇ e aksharrūp je mukta temnī panktimā bhaḷvu chhe ane Akshardhāmmā jaīne akhand Bhagwānnī sevāmā hajūr rahevu chhe, paṇ nāshvant ne tuchchha evu je māyik sukh tene ichchhvu nathī ne emā koī ṭhekāṇe lobhāvu nathī;' evo dṛuḍh nishchay rākhīne nirantar Bhagwānnī ekāntik bhakti karvī.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 147-149
---------------------
 - Kartā'yam sarva-hartā'yam sarvopari niyāmakah | Pratyaksham-iha labdho me Swāminārāyaṇo Harih || 147 ||
 - Ata evā'smi dhanyo'ham parama-bhāgyavān-aham | Kṛutārthash-chaiva nihshanko nishchinto'smi sadā sukhī || 148 ||
 - Evam prāpter-mahimnash-cha pratyaham parichintanam | Prabhoh prasannatāyāsh-cha kāryam sthireṇa chetasā || 149 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.27
  Ā pṛuthvī jenī rākhī sthir rahī chhe ne ḍolāvī ḍole chhe tathā ā tārāmanḍaḷ jenu rākhyu adhar rahyu chhe tathā jenā varsāvyā megh varshe chhe tathā jenī āgnāe karīne sūrya-chandra uday-astapaṇāne pāme chhe...evā anant āshcharya chhe te sarve mane maḷyā evā je Bhagwān tenā karyā ja thāy chhe,' em samaje, paṇ pragaṭ pramāṇ je Bhagwān te vinā bījo koī e āshcharyano karnāro chhe em māne nahī.
• Vach.Ga.Pr.58
  Atishay je moṭāpurush hoy teno je upar rājīpo thāy tenā game tevā malin sanskār hoy to nāsh pāme ane moṭāpurushno rājīpo thayo hoy to rānk hoy te rājā thāy ane game tevā bhūnḍā prārabdha hoy to rūḍā thāy ane game tevu tene māthe vighna āvnāru hoy te nāsh thaī jāy.

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 4/9
  Hamaṇā jaṇātu nathī, paṇ āpaṇne Bhagwān maḷyā chhe māṭe kṛutārtha thayā chhīe.

Class 61
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 240
---------------------
 - Svāminārāyaṇe bhaktim parām dṛuḍhayitum hṛudi | Guruhareḥ samādeshāchchāturmāsye vratam charet || 240 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 241-242
---------------------
 - Chāndrāyaṇopavāsādirmantrajapaḥ pradakshiṇāḥ | Kathāshrutirdaṇḍavachcha praṇāmā adhikāstadā || 241 ||
 - Ityādirūpe shraddhayā prītipūrvakam | Hariprasannatām prāptum visheṣhām bhaktimācharet || 242 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 243
---------------------
 - Sampradāyasya shāstrāṇām paṭhanam pāṭhanam tadā | Yathāruchi yathāshakti kuryād niyamapūrvakam || 243 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 244
---------------------
 - Sarvaiḥ satsangibhiḥ kāryāḥ prītim vardhayitum harau | Utsavā bhaktibhāvena harṣheṇollāsatastathā || 244 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 245
---------------------
 - Janmamahotsavā nityam svāminārāyaṇaprabhoḥ | Brahmā'ksharagurūṇām cha kartavyā bhaktibhāvataḥ || 245 ||

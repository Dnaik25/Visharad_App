Class 64
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 258-259
---------------------
 - Svāminārāyaṇeneha svayam yaddhi prasāditam | Gurubhishchā'kṣharabrahmasvarūpairyat prasāditam || 258 ||
 - Teṣhām sthānavisheṣhāṇām yātrām kartum ya ichchhati | Tadyātrām sa janaḥ kuryād yathāshakti yathāruchi || 259 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 260
---------------------
 - Ayodhyām mathurām kāshīm kedāram badarīm vrajet | Rāmeshvarādi tīrtham cha yathāshakti yathāruchi || 260 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 261
---------------------
 - Maryādā pālanīyaiva sarvairmandiramāgataiḥ | Nāryo naiva naraiḥ spṛushyā nārībhishcha narāstathā || 261 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 262
---------------------
 - Niyamamanusṛutyaiva satsangasya tu mandire | Vastrāṇi paridheyāni strībhiḥ pumbhishcha sarvadā || 262 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 263
---------------------
 - Gachchhed yadā darshanārtham bhaktajano harerguroḥ | Riktena pāṇinā naiva gachchhet tadā kadāchana || 263 ||

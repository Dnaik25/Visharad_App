Class 32
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 120
---------------------
 - Parasmād Brahmaṇo'nyasmin-naksharād Brahmaṇas-tathā | Prītyabhāvo hi vairāgyam angam bhakteh sahāyakam || 120 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 121
---------------------
 - Nindā-lajjā-bhayā'padbhyah satsangam na parityajet | Swāminārāyaṇam Devam tad-bhaktim karhichid-gurum || 121 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 122
---------------------
 - Sevā Haresh-cha bhaktānām kartavyā shuddha-bhāvatah | Mahad-bhāgyam mamāstīti matvā sva-moksha-hetunā || 122 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.28
  Amāre to Bhagwānno rājīpo thayā sāru janmojanm Bhagwānnā bhaktnī ja sevā karvī chhe.
• Vach.Ga.Ma.59
  Bhagwānnā santnī sevā to bahu ja moṭā puṇyavāḷāne maḷe chhe paṇ thoḍā puṇyavāḷāne maḷtī nathī.
• Vach.Ga.Ma.41
  Jene Parameshwar bhajvā hoy tene Bhagwānnī athavā Bhagwānnā bhaktnī sevā-chākarī maḷe tyāre potānu moṭu bhāgya mānīne sevā karvī, te paṇ Bhagwānnī prasannatāne arthe ne potānā kalyāṇne arthe bhaktie karīne ja karvī, paṇ koī vakhāṇe te sāru na karvī.

Kirtan
------
• Muktanand Swami
  Dāsī thaī raheje tu dīnadayāḷnī...
  Dāsī thaī raheje tu dīnadayāḷnī, Nīchī ṭel maḷe to māne bhāgya jo; Bhavabrahmādikne nishche maḷtī nathī, Purushottam pāse beṭhānī jāgya jo... sāmbhaḷ 2

Satsang Diksha Shlok 123
---------------------
 - Neyo na vyarthatām kālah satsanga-bhajanam vinā | Ālasyam cha pramādādi parityājyam hi sarvadā || 123 ||

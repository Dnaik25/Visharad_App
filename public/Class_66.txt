Satsang Diksha Shlok 277
---------------------
 - Kālo vā karma vā māyā prabhavennaiva karhichit | Aniṣhṭakaraṇe nūnam satsangā' 'shrayashālinām || 277 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.72
  Je bhagwānnā bhaktane bhagwānno nishchay māhātmya sahit hoy ne santnu ne satsangīnu māhātmya ghaṇu jāṇto hoy, ne te bhaktnu jo karma kaṭhaṇ hoy ne kāḷ paṇ kaṭhaṇ hoy to paṇ te bhaktane evī bhaktinu atishay baḷ chhe, te kāḷ ne karma e bey maḷīne tenu bhūnḍu karī shaktā nathī.

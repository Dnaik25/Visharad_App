Class 72
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 305
---------------------
 - Prashāntairjāyatām yukto manuṣhyairdharmashālibhiḥ | Samsāraḥ sādhanāshīlairadhyātmamārgasamsthitaiḥ || 305 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 306
---------------------
 - Aikyam mithaḥ suhṛudbhāvo maitrī kāruṇyameva cha | Sahanashīlatā snehaḥ sarvajaneṣhu vardhatām || 306 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 307
---------------------
 - Satsange divyasambandhād brahmaṇaḥ parabrahmaṇaḥ | Sarveṣhām jāyatām dārḍhyam nirdoṣhadivyabhāvayoḥ || 307 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Ma.63
  Bhagwānnā bhakta chhe te to kevaḷ brahmanī ja murtio chhe, ene viṣhe to manuṣhyabhāv lāvvo ja nahī.

Purushottam Bolya Prite
-----------------------
• Purushottam Bolya Prite
  Bhagwānne bhajtā sarve harijano divya chhe. Ā marmnī vāt je koī samaje, dhāre ne vichāre chhe te magna thaī jāy chhe ne jag jītī jāy chhe. Āṭho pahar ānand eno ṭaḷto nathī.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 308
---------------------
 - Akshararūpatām sarve samprāpya svātmani janāḥ | Prāpnuyuḥ sahajānande bhaktim hi puruṣhottame || 308 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 309-310
---------------------
 - Māghasya shuklapanchamyām ārabdhamasya lekhanam | Pavitre vikramābde hi rasaṛṣhikhadvisammite || 309 ||
 - Chaitrashuklanavamyām cha svāminārāyaṇaprabhoḥ | Tachcha sampūrṇatām prāptam divyajanmamahotsave || 310 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 311-314
---------------------
 - Upāsyasahajānanda-haraye parabrahmaṇe | Mūlā'kṣharaguṇātītānandāya svāmine tathā || 311 ||
 - Bhagatajīmahārāja-sākshādvignānamūrtaye | Yagnapuruṣhadāsāya satyasiddhāntarakshiṇe || 312 ||
 - Vātsalyā'rdrā'tmane nityam ānandabrahmayogine | Vishvavandyavinamrāya gurave pramukhāya cha || 313 ||
 - Anjaliḥ shāstrarūpo'yam sānandam bhaktibhāvataḥ | Arpyate pramukhasvāmi-janmashatābdiparvaṇi || 314 ||

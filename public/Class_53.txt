Class 53
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 193
---------------------
 - Lunchā kadāpi na grāhyā kaishchid-api janairiha | Naiva kāryo vyayo vyarthah kāryah svā'yā'nusāratah || 193 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 194
---------------------
 - Kartavyam lekhanam samyak svasyā'yasya vyayasya cha | Niyamān-anusṛutyaiva prashāsana-kṛutān sadā || 194 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 195
---------------------
 - Svā'yāddhi dashamo bhāgo vimsho'thavā sva-shaktitah | Arpyah sevā-prasādārtham Swāminārāyaṇa-prabhoh || 195 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 196
---------------------
 - Svopayogā'nusāreṇa prakuryāt sangraham gṛuhī | Anna-dravya-dhanādīnām kāla-shakty-anusāratah || 196 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 197
---------------------
 - Anna-phalādibhish-chaiva yathā-shakti jalādibhih | Pālitāh pashu-pakshyādyāh sambhāvyā hi yathochitam || 197 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 198
---------------------
 - Dhana-dravya-dharādīnām pradānā'dānayoh punah | Vishvāsa-hananam naiva kāryam na kapaṭam tathā || 198 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 199
---------------------
 - Pradātum karma-kāribhyah pratignātam dhanādikam | Yathā-vācham pradeyam tad no'nam deyam kadāchana || 199 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 200
---------------------
 - Naiva vishvāsa-ghātam hi kuryāt satsangam-āshritah | Pālayed vachanam dattam pratignātam na langhayet || 200 ||

Class 56
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 213
---------------------
 - Visheṣhasamyamaḥ pālyaḥ kaumārye yauvane tathā | Ayogyasparshadṛushyādyāstyājyāḥ shaktivināshakāḥ || 213 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.8
  Jo indriyonī vṛuttione strīādiknā viṣhaymā pravartāve chhe to enu antaḥkaraṇ bhraṣhṭ thāy chhe ane kalyāṇnā mārg thakī paḍī jāy chhe.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 214
---------------------
 - Satphalonnāyakam kuryād uchitameva sāhasam | Na kuryāt kevalam yaddhi svamanolokaranjakam || 214 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 215
---------------------
 - Niyatodyamakartavye nā' 'lasyam āpnuyāt kvachit | Shraddhām prītim harau kuryāt pūjām satsangamanvaham || 215 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.20
  Haribhaktane to jeṭlī kasar rahe chhe teṭlī potāne āḷase karīne rahe chhe.
• Vach.Sā.18
  Je shraddhāvān puruṣh hoy ane tene jo sāchā santno sang maḷe ane te santnā vachanne viṣhe shraddhāvān thāy, to enā hṛudayne viṣhe svadharma, vairāgya, vivek, gnān, bhakti ādik je kalyāṇkārī guṇ te sarve prakaṭ thaī āve chhe ane kām-krodhādik je vikār te baḷī jāy chhe.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 216
---------------------
 - Sango'tra balavān loke yathāsangam hi jīvanam | Satām sangam ataḥ kuryātkusangam sarvathā tyajet || 216 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa.Vato.4/124
  Sangno bhed em chhe je, so janme ekāntik thavāno hoy te āj thāy ane ā janme ekāntik thavāno hoy tene paṇ so janma dharvā paḍe; e be bhed chhe. Tyā dṛuṣhṭānt dīdhu je, lākaḍu ne pāṇo ekbījāne tāre ne buḍāḍe.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 217
---------------------
 - Kāmā'sakto bhaved yo hi kṛutaghnolokavanchakaḥ | Pākhaṇḍī kapaṭī yashcha tasya sangam parityajet || 217 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 218-219
---------------------
 - Harestadavatārāṇām khaṇḍanam vidadhāti yaḥ | Upāsteḥ khaṇḍanam yashcha kurute paramātmanaḥ || 218 ||
 - Khaṇḍanam mandirāṇām yo mūrtīnām kurute hareḥ | Satyā'himsādidharmāṇām tasya sangam parityajet || 219 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 220
---------------------
 - Sākṛutikam parabrahma manute yo nirākṛuti | Tasya sango na kartavyastādṛuggranthān paṭhennahi || 220 ||

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.Pr.68
  Mārī aṣhṭ prakārnī je pratimā tathā je sant tene viṣhe hu akhaṇḍ nivās karīne rahu chhu.

Class 41
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 156-157
---------------------
 - Sva-dharmam pālayen-nityam para-dharmam parityajet | Sva-dharmo Bhagavad-gurvor-āgnāyāh paripālanam || 156 ||
 - Tad-āgnām yat parityajya kriyate sva-manodhṛutam | Para-dharmah sa vigneyo vivekibhir-mumukshubhih || 157 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 4/119
  Keṭlākne man ramāḍe chhe ne keṭlāk manne ramāḍe chhe. Ā vāt nitye vichārvā jevī chhe.
• Swa. Vato: 2/57
  Sant kahe tem karavu te shreṣhṭh chhe ne mandhāryu karavu te kaniṣhṭh chhe, ne mangamtu karato hoy ne te tyāg rākhato hoy ne ākhā mandirnu kām ekalo karato hoy ne game eṭalā māṇasne satsang karāvto hoy, to paṇ te nyūn chhe ne tene koīk divas vighna chhe. Ane je traṇ ṭāṇā khāto hoy ne āḷasu hoy ne ūnghato hoy evī rītnā doṣhe yukta hoy, paṇ jo te potānu mangamtu mūkīne sant kahe tem kare to te adhik chhe. Ne sant kahe em karavu e nirguṇ chhe ne mangamtu karavu e saguṇ chhe.

Purushottam Bolya Prite
-----------
• Purushottam Bolya Prite
  Ingoralama Shri Harie kahyu: Amara janoe manmukhi na thavu pan agnama rahevu. Manmukhi thai te bhale anek dharma pale, ke mahavairagyavalo hoi, ke maro naam le, ke maro mahima kahe, ke mane sambharine roi jai, sharir sukvi nakhe, ke ahonish dhyan kare ke kavya, kirtan kare, topan marathi vimukh chhe.

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Va.1
  Je kalyāṇne ichchhe tene to man sāthe jarūr vair bāndhvu.

Vachanvidhi
-----------
• Vachanvidhi - 4/5,7
  So vātnī ek vāt chhe, nav karvo āgnālop; Rājī karvānu rahyu paru, paṇ karāviye nahi Harine kop. Alp sukh sāru āganyā, lope chhe Shrīharitaṇī; Param sukh kem pāmshe, bhāī dhārjo tenā dhaṇī.

Sar-Siddhi
----------
• Sar-Siddhi - 28/9
  Dharma dharma sahu koī kahe, paṇ dharmamā bahu marm chhe; Pragaṭ Prabhunā vachan pāḷe, ethī moṭo koī dharma chhe.

Satsang Diksha Shlok 158
---------------------
 - Satsanga-niyamād yaddhi viruddham dharma-lopakam | Yat-karma phala-dam vā'pi tad-ācharyam na karhichit || 158 ||

Swamini Vato
------------
• Swa. Vato: 6/156
  Ek vār Mahārāje ūbhā thaīne kahyu hatu je, koī niyam bhang karsho mā, ne jene karvo hoy te satsangmā rahesho mā, ne ā je sūraj sarkhī godaḍiyu temā bhalā thaīne ḍāgh lāgvā desho mā.

Vachanvidhi
-----------
• Vachanvidhi - 42/2
  Ṭhelī niyamne kām na karvu, Mar hoy lābh jo lākhno; Toy lalchāviye nahi lesh manne, Jāṇiye mavāḷo kākhno.

Bhaktichintamani
----------------
• Bhaktichintamani - 76/39-41
  Sukh duhkha āve sarve bheḷu, temā rākhjyo sthir mati; Jāḷvīsh mārā janne, vaḷī karīsh jatan ati. Em kartā jo panḍ paḍshe, to āgaḷ sukh chhe ati ghaṇu; Paṇ vrat ṭek jo ṭāḷsho to, bhogavsho sahu sahu taṇu. Nahi to tame nachint rahejyo, karvu tamāre kāī nathī; Je maḷyā chhe tamne, te pār chhe aksharthī.

Class 49
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 180
---------------------
 - Kudṛuṣhṭyā purushair-naiva striyo dṛushyāh kadāchana | Evam-eva kudṛuṣhṭyā cha strībhir-dṛushyā na pūrushāh || 180 ||

Title: Drushtino Sanyam ane pavitrata

Vachanamrut
-----------
• Vach.Ga.An.22
  Upar kudṛuṣhṭie jovāyu hoy to tenu vadhu pāp chhe...Satsangī harijan chhe te to jevā potānā mā, ben ke dīkrī hoy tevā chhe...Je harijanne viṣhe kudṛuṣhṭi rākhe te atishay pāpī chhe ne teno koī kāḷe chhūṭko thāy nahī.

Kirtan
------
• Premanand Swami
  Bāio dekhīne bhāio...
  Bāio dekhīne bhāio, chheṭerā chāle, Paḍī vastu koīnī, hāthe nav jhāle rāj...

Class 62
======

Satsang Diksha Shlok 246
---------------------
 - Harergurorvishiṣhṭānām prasangānām dineṣhu cha | Satsangibhiryathāshakti kāryāḥ parvotsavājanaiḥ || 246 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 247
---------------------
 - Savādyam kīrtanam kāryam parvotsaveṣhu bhaktitaḥ | Mahimnashcha kathāvārtā karaṇīyā visheṣhataḥ || 247 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 248
---------------------
 - Chaitrashuklanavamyām hi kāryam shrīrāmapūjanam | Kṛuṣhṇā'ṣhṭamyām tu kartavyam shrāvaṇe kṛuṣhṇapūjanam || 248 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 249
---------------------
 - Shivarātrau hi kartavyam pūjanam shankarasya cha | Gaṇesham bhādrashuklāyām chaturthyām pūjayet tathā || 249 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 250
---------------------
 - Mārutim āshvine kṛuṣhṇachaturdashyām hi pūjayet | Mārge mandirasamprāptau taddevam praṇamed hṛudā || 250 ||

Satsang Diksha Shlok 251
---------------------
 - Viṣhṇushcha shankarashchaiva pārvatī cha gajānanaḥ | Dinakarashcha panchaitā mānyāḥ pūjyā hi devatāḥ || 251 ||
